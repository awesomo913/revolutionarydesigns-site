Common questions

Answers before you call.

Do you charge for the design visit?

The initial on-site visit and concept are free. Detailed plans and 3D renderings for larger builds carry a fee that we credit back if you hire us.

Why do hardscapes sink or shift?

Almost always base preparation and drainage. We excavate deeper and compact in lifts, which is invisible when done right and obvious when it is not.

When should I start planning a big build?

Spring and summer fills up fast. Reach out in late winter if you want the work done that summer.
