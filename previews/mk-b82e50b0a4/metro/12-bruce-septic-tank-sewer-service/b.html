Common questions

Before you call, the honest answers.

How often should my tank be pumped?

Every 3 to 5 years for a typical household. More often if you have a garbage disposal or a large family.

What counts as an emergency?

Sewage backing into the house, a tank overflowing, or a pump alarm. Call immediately - that is a health issue, not a maintenance item.

Do you handle the permits?

Yes. For new installations we handle the permit application and inspection scheduling with the county.
