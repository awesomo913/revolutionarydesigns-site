Questions

Frequently asked.

When is the best time of year to pave?

Late spring through early fall, while the mix stays hot enough to compact properly. Sealcoating needs warm, dry weather to cure.

How long before I can drive on it?

Usually 24 to 48 hours for a driveway, longer in cool weather. We tell you exactly when before we leave.

Can you match my existing driveway?

In most cases yes. Bring us a photo and the approximate age and we will tell you honestly whether a patch will blend or a full overlay looks better.
