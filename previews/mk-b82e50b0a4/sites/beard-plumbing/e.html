Common questions

Answers before you call.

Do you charge for estimates?

Diagnostic visits are quoted upfront before we start - you approve the number before any work happens.

My water heater is leaking - is that an emergency?

If it is actively leaking from the tank itself, yes, shut the water off and call. Tank leaks rarely repair and usually mean replacement.

Can you help with low water pressure?

That is usually a failing fixture cartridge, a partially closed valve, or buildup in galvanized pipe. A quick diagnostic tells us which.
