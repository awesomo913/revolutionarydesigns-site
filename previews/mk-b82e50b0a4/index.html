How to use these on a call or visit

  1. Pick the business, open its compare page — all six directions load together.
  2. Lead with the one that fits them: A/D for emergency & photo-forward trades, B/E for homeowner-trust and multi-service trades, C/F for design-led / high-ticket work.
  3. Every page has a working quote form and click-to-call — it already feels like their site.
  4. Reviews and job photos are honest placeholders, not real data — swap in theirs once they're in.
