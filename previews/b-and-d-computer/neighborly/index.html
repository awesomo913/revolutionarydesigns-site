How it works

A repair actually goes like this.

01

You bring it in, or you call

Walk in during shop hours or call ahead. Bernie looks at most machines the same day — no dropping it off and waiting a week to hear back.

02

You get a real number

Free estimate, before anything is opened up. If the fix costs more than the computer is worth, you'll hear that too.

03

You get it back running

Most repairs go same-day or next-day. Parts for common jobs — screens, batteries, drives — are usually already on the shelf.

What we do

Services, plainly.

  • Computer repair & diagnostics

    Desktops and laptops, any brand. We find the actual problem before we quote you anything.

  • Virus & malware removal

    Slow, popping, or hijacked — cleaned out and set up so it doesn't come back as easily.

  • Upgrades — RAM, SSD, GPU, CPU

    Make what you own faster instead of buying new. Most upgrades done same day.

  • Custom PC builds

    Gaming, workstation, or office — built for what you actually use it for, not whatever's on sale.

  • Laptop repair

    Screens, batteries, keyboards, hinges. Common parts kept in stock.

  • New & used computers

    Warrantied and set up before you leave the shop. Priced for a real budget.

  • Data recovery

    Hard drives, SSDs, USB drives. If the data's recoverable, we'll tell you honestly whether to try.

  • Home & small-business networking

    Wi-Fi dead zones, new routers, small office setups. Printer setup too.
