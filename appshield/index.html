03 · Why trust this

What is this?

This checker is the free front door of AppShield, a pre-flight compliance linter for app store submissions. Its rulebook is a registry of 86 rules where every store-cited rule carries the exact guideline reference and a link to the official source, re-verified against Apple, Google, Microsoft, Amazon and Samsung policy pages. Rules that are engineering best practice rather than store law are labeled that way instead of dressed up as one.

Honest limits: a static checker cannot tell you whether your app will pass review. Two dozen requirement types (metadata, screenshots, privacy labels, runtime behavior) are invisible to source scanning. Here is the full list of what this can and can't catch.
