What is this?

This checker is the free front door of AppShield, a pre-flight compliance linter for app-store submissions. Its rulebook is a registry of 86 rules where every store-cited rule carries the exact guideline reference and a link to the official source, re-verified against Apple / Google / Microsoft / Amazon / Samsung policy pages — and rules that are engineering best practice rather than store law are labeled that way instead of dressed up.

Honesty first: a static checker cannot tell you whether your app will pass review — two dozen requirement types (metadata, screenshots, privacy labels, runtime behavior) are invisible to source scanning. Here's the full list of what this can and can't catch.
