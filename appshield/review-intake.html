After you order

Send these and the clock starts

Send these and the clock starts. Anything missing pauses the 24 to 48 hour window until it arrives.

What to send

  1. Build accessA TestFlight invite, an APK, an internal testing link, or a screen recording if a build genuinely cannot be shared. The review needs the actual build, not a description of it.
  2. Draft store listing textTitle, subtitle, description, and category, as you intend to submit them.
  3. Screenshots and any preview videoChecked against what the build actually shows.
  4. Draft privacy label or Data Safety answersCompared against what the app is observed to collect.
  5. Age rating questionnaire answersCompared against what's actually visible on screen.
  6. Privacy policy URLIt needs to be live and reachable, not a placeholder.
  7. An ordinary test account loginA normal test account, never a production customer account.
  8. IAP and subscription productsPrices, billing period, and where in the app the paywall appears.
  9. Account creation and deletion stepsHow a reviewer creates an account, and how they'd delete one.
  10. Reviewer notesAnything a store reviewer would need to get into and understand the app.
  11. Any previous rejection messageSo the same issue doesn't get flagged twice.

Never send

Never send App Store Connect or Google Play Console credentials, signing keys, or production customer accounts. I will not accept them, and I will ask you to rotate anything sent by mistake.

How to send it

  • Reply to the order confirmation emailwith the list above, item by item.
  • Use a shared folder link for large assetsscreen recordings, screenshot sets, and preview video, rather than attaching them directly.
  • Do not paste secrets into the email bodylogins, keys, and tokens belong nowhere in plain text, including here.

What happens next

  1. Intake complete

    Every item above has arrived and the 24 to 48 hour window starts.

  2. Review begins

    The build, listing, and declarations are checked item by item against what the app actually does.

  3. Report delivered

    Written report delivered as PDF and Markdown, within 24 to 48 hours of complete intake.

  4. One round of written clarification

    Follow-up questions on the report, answered in writing.

  5. One focused recheck

    Within 7 days, scoped to the items flagged in the original report.
