Google Play update blocked? The August 31, 2026 requirements

If Play Console is rejecting your upload around this date, it is almost always one of two things: your app's target API level or your Play Billing Library version. Both requirements take effect August 31, 2026, and both can be pushed to November 1, 2026 with an extension request in Play Console.

The two requirements

1. Target API level 36 (Android 16)

Google requires most apps and updates to target API level 36. If your app's targetSdkVersion or targetSdk is set below 36 in your gradle configuration, Play Console will block the upload with a target API level error. The exact fix depends on your framework: see our dedicated guides for Flutter, React Native, or Expo.

2. Play Billing Library v8 minimum

If your app uses Google Play's billing system for in-app purchases or subscriptions, Google requires Play Billing Library version 8 or higher. Apps still depending on com.android.billingclient:billing v6 or v7 will be blocked. See our dedicated guide: Billing Library v8 migration.

Requesting the November 1, 2026 extension in Play Console

Google offers a short extension window, pushing the effective deadline to November 1, 2026, for developers who need more time. This is requested directly inside Play Console against the specific app and requirement that is blocking you, rather than granted automatically. If your upload is rejected and you need more runway than you have left before August 31, check your app's dashboard in Play Console for the extension request option tied to that specific policy requirement, and complete it before your existing submission window closes.

Checklist

  • Confirm the actual error. Read the exact Play Console rejection message. It names either the target API level requirement or the Billing Library requirement.
  • Check your target SDK. Look at android/app/build.gradle (Flutter), android/build.gradle (React Native), or your Expo SDK version / expo-build-properties config, and confirm it resolves to 36.
  • Check your billing dependency. If you use Play Billing, confirm com.android.billingclient resolves to 8.0.0 or higher.
  • Rebuild and retest on Android 16 before resubmitting, since target-API behavior changes only surface at runtime.
  • If you can't finish in time, request the extension in Play Console now rather than waiting until August 31.
