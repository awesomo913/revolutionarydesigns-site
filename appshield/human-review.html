What I review

  • Store listing claims vs actual app behaviorDoes what the listing promises match what the app does.
  • Screenshot accuracyWhether screenshots reflect the current build and don't mislead.
  • Privacy labels / Data Safety answers vs observed collectionWhat you declared against what the app actually collects.
  • Age-rating answers vs visible contentWhether the questionnaire answers match what's on screen.
  • In-app purchase, subscription, restore, and cancellation flowsWhether pricing display and controls hold up in practice.
  • Account creation and deletionWhether the required paths exist and work as described.
  • Login and reviewer accessWhether a reviewer can actually get into the app to test it.
  • User-generated-content safeguards, where applicableReporting, blocking, and moderation paths if your app has UGC.
  • Broken links, placeholder content, dead endsThe kind of thing that stalls a review cycle for no good reason.
  • Store-specific declarations and reviewer notesWhether what you'll submit to the reviewer matches what's true.

What you send

  • Draft privacy-label or Data Safety answers
