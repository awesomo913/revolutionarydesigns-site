Frequently asked

Why do I need closed testing at all?

Google Play requires most new apps to run a closed test with a minimum number of real, opted-in testers for a set number of days before they can apply for production access. The exact requirement is on Google's own page, linked above.

Who are the testers?

Developers in our community exchange who opt in to test other developers' apps. You test back for others during the same window—it's a real reciprocal cohort, not a purchased list.

Does this guarantee I'll get production access?

No. Google makes that decision. This package gets your testing requirement met with real testers and gives your app a preflight and application review first, which is the part within our control.

How long does the closed-testing window last?

Fourteen days, tracked daily, matching the exchange's standard cohort window.

Can I buy just the closed-testing coordination without the preflight?

The Launch Package is priced as the pair. If you only need the preflight, see the $99 pilot review.

What if I already have testers?

Tell us during scoping. If your existing cohort already satisfies the requirement, we'll say so rather than sell you testers you don't need.

Not sure if you already meet the testing requirement? Describe your app and current testing setup and we'll confirm what, if anything, is still missing before you send anything.
