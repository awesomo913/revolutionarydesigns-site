A correction is a proposal until it is checked.

The sample checklist leaves every item Awaiting implementation / not rechecked. In a real focused recheck, the report records the submitted correction, the evidence inspected, and whether the original condition is supported, still open, or not evaluated.

Only originally flagged items are covered. New builds or broader changes require the written scope to be confirmed; a reviewed screenshot does not establish unrelated behavior or backend handling.

What this sample cannot establish

No real client build was exercised. The illustrations do not establish backend deletion, full data handling, purchase behavior, other devices, or an app-store decision. A paid review records its actual environment, reachable flows, evidence, and limitations.
