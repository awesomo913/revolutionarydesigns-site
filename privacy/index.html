Website privacy.

This notice covers the Revolutionary Designs website and the free website-audit form. Product-specific information is available in the AppShield privacy notice.

Your audit request

The form sends your business name, website, email, optional phone number, and project message to our Cloudflare Worker so I can receive and reply to your request. Cloudflare also processes ordinary network information needed to operate and secure the endpoint. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive records.

Your details are used for the inquiry and related project correspondence. Sending an audit request does not subscribe you to marketing emails. Correspondence may be retained to follow up and keep a record of our conversation. You can ask what is held about your inquiry or request deletion using the email below; any record that needs to be kept for an active project or transaction will be explained.

Hosting and measurement

GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process information such as your IP address, browser, requested page, and request time. Some pages, including the homepage, load PostHog to measure page views, interactions, referrers, device type, and approximate geographic information.

Analytics uses memory-only identifiers and session recording is disabled. We do not intentionally save a PostHog identifier in cookies or local storage across visits. Providers still receive normal network information. The audit form is excluded from automatic interaction capture; its field values are not deliberately included in our analytics events.

Demo pages and saved games

The store demo keeps its sample cart on your device when browser storage is available. Sample checkout and roofing requests do not send orders or inquiries and do not take payments. You can reset the sample cart from the demo. Games can save progress on your device; each product explains its own save options.

Contact

For questions about website inquiries or your information, email hello@revolutionarydesigns.io or call (502) 642-0012.
