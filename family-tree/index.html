How this was researched

Every person and date on this page traces to a record fetched and checked against at least one independent source during research in October 2026. Where sources disagree — a gravestone against a family history, one county index against another — both versions are recorded and the conflict is flagged on the person’s card, never silently resolved.

The tree was built from free public records, including:

  • Find A Grave — memorial pages, family sections, and cemetery rosters (including the Emerson family farm cemetery in Pickaway County, Ohio, and the Spaw cemetery in Casey County, Kentucky)
  • KYGenWeb — the Casey County marriage index (1800–1900), cemetery lists, and obituaries
  • WikiTree — linked family profiles, used to follow the Spaw, Gilpin, Luttrell, Turpin, and Wesley lines
  • FamilySearch — tree records for the Emerson, Rainwater, and Daws families
  • WeRelate, archive.org (the 1906 History of Pickaway County, Ohio), the Rainwater one-name study, and the compiled Turner/Phelps family history

Living family members appear by name only, with no dates or contact details. This page is kept out of search engines on purpose.
