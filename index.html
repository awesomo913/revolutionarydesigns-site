What is included?

Structure, copy, responsive design, development, and launch checks are included in the agreed build. Your quote lists the pages, products, integrations, and content required. Extra pages, custom features, photography, and third-party subscriptions are discussed before they add to the price.

How long does it take?

We set a delivery schedule once the scope, content, and access are clear. You receive the milestones in your quote, including what I need from you to keep the project moving.

How do revisions and payments work?

Your written scope includes the review stages, revision allowance, and payment milestones. Requests that change the scope are quoted before additional work begins.

Who owns the domain and site?

Your domain and business accounts belong to you. Ownership, access, and the source or platform handoff are documented before the build. Site Care is optional; cancellation does not remove your ownership.

What changes the quote?

Page and product counts, booking systems, migration, custom workflows, and integrations can change the scope. Payment-provider fees and other third-party costs are listed separately. The starting prices are not a promise of unlimited features.
