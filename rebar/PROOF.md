# Rebar Takeoff — Proof Sheet

1. **What this is:** a web page that works out how much rebar a concrete slab or foundation needs.

2. **What it does for you:**
   - You draw the slab by typing each side, or by tapping its corners on a photo.
   - It finds the square feet, even for odd shapes with curves.
   - It takes out the holes, like old foundations and piers.
   - It adds the overlap where two 40-foot bars meet.
   - It gives the pounds and tons of steel, and shows every step of the math.

3. **How it was made:** the designer designed it. AI helped build it.

4. **What it costs and gives back:** it is free to use. Nothing you type or photograph leaves your device. Your job saves on your own device, and you can download a job file as a backup.

5. **Who is responsible:** the site owner. Last reviewed October 30, 2026.

6. **Proof it works:**
   - 38 automatic math checks pass. Ten use made-up slabs worked out by hand. Twenty-four check the screen math and the warnings. Four come from real takeoffs done by hand in a notebook, and the tool gets the same answers.
   - A separate review hunted for places where a mistake could slip by without a warning. It found ten, and all ten were fixed and re-checked.
   - Screenshots of the page on a laptop and a phone were checked by eye.

7. **Changelog:**
   - October 30, 2026: first version. Draw by sides or photo, cutouts, laps every 40 feet, two ways to weigh the steel, job totals, print and spreadsheet export.
