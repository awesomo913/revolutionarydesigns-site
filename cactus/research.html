How this reference is built

Recent research notes are used to locate sources, then the public record is checked before a finding is included here. A real DOI establishes that a publication exists; it does not establish that a summary interpreted it correctly.

Botanical profiles identify the taxonomic treatment used. Grower records are attributed to their author. Experiments name their study host. Care guidance distinguishes a reasonable horticultural practice from a tested outcome.

What changed in this edition

  • Taxonomic synonyms are separated from clone identity. Lophophora species counts now follow one explicit, dated treatment.
  • Shared BK and JS source numbers are no longer treated as universal clone aliases.
  • The pest guide includes root inspections, life-stage considerations, phytotoxicity testing and limits on transferring trials between hosts.
  • The health guide separates physiological injury, fungi, bacteria, oomycetes and viruses.
  • Fixed frost thresholds, universal fertilizer calendars and untested growth multipliers have been removed from advice.
  • Graft alignment allows vascular overlap with unequal diameters and an offset position.
  • The conservation guide corrects blanket claims about cultivated-plant and hybrid exemptions.

What remains open

The full community name registry contains many entries whose original source or clone chain has not been authenticated for this publication. Historical origin stories, unresolved aliases and metadata-only treatment leads are not promoted into facts here.

Some scientific literature is available only as a title or abstract. This edition does not claim to have verified every archived post, every registry identity or every paper in full. A missing search result is recorded as a search limitation, not proof that research or a specimen does not exist.
