# Store demonstration photography

`store-still-life.webp` is illustrative product photography generated with the built-in image tool for the fictional Field & Form store demo. It does not depict real inventory or a customer business. The demo explicitly labels this imagery and its sample orders.

Prompt: premium natural-daylight homeware still life on warm ivory stone; an ivory ceramic mug on the left, nested sand ceramic bowls in the center, folded flax linen tea towel on the right; neutral background, soft shadows, material detail, no text, brands or extra objects. Landscape composition with negative space above.

Original generated PNG is retained in the local generated-images folder. The WebP is an encoding optimization for delivery; product crops are CSS presentation of the same photograph.
