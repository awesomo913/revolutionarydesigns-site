How this was researched

Every person and date on this page traces to a record fetched and checked against at least one independent source during research in October 2026. Where sources disagree — a gravestone against a county index, one census reading against another — both versions are recorded and the conflict is flagged on the person’s card, never silently resolved.

The tree was built from free public records, including:

  • Find A Grave — memorial pages and family sections, including the Pullen graves in Rockwall and Dallas counties, Texas, and the Hart graves at Big Clifty, Kentucky
  • USGenWeb Archives (Breckinridge County, Kentucky) — the 1850 census, the 1855–57 and 1883–96 marriage books, and county court and cemetery transcriptions, recovered through the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine after the original site went offline
  • The National Archives — the 1950 U.S. Census (Willard and Rose Marie Pullen, Campbell County, Kentucky)
  • The SSA Numident index and Kentucky vital-record indexes (via sortedbyname.com) — Social Security and state records that name parents; Willard’s record names Rannie Pullen and Eva L. West
  • The family’s own records — the Ancestry tree and screenshots supplied in October 2026, used for the Jordan, Elliott, Schooler, Collins and allied lines, and flagged on the cards where not yet independently verified

Living family members appear by name only, with no dates or contact details. This page is kept out of search engines on purpose.
